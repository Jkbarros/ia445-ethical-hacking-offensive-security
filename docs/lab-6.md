Explain why either hping3 or HOIC have legitimate uses for offensive security? Give examples.

Hping3: Primarily a network tool for crafting and sending custom TCP/IP packets, it is used in network security auditing, penetration testing, and network performance testing. It helps in identifying vulnerabilities, testing network defenses, and assessing how systems handle various network conditions.

HOIC: Known for its capacity to generate significant load on web servers, it's utilized in stress testing and DDoS mitigation testing. It allows organizations to evaluate the resilience of web applications under heavy traffic and the effectiveness of their DDoS countermeasures.

Both tools are also valuable in educational contexts for teaching cybersecurity concepts. Their use should always be ethical, legal, and with explicit permission to avoid legal and ethical issues.
