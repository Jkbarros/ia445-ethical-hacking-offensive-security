## Page 1

Until 2019, I had little awareness of cyber-attacks and the crucial role of cybersecurity. However, 
everything changed in November 2019 when my dad fell victim to a cyber-attack on his company's 
bank account. Hackers executed numerous unauthorized transactions, resulting in significant 
financial losses. When we approached the bank, his manager explained that cyberattacks were 
becoming increasingly prevalent each day. Shockingly, he revealed that the breach was partly the 
bank's fault due to a lack of security in their mobile app. 
 
This incident marked a turning point for me, prompting me to delve into the realm of cybersecurity. 
At that time, I encountered a challenge – there were no Brazilian YouTube channels or blogs 
providing information or study materials on this subject. This realization also underscored the 
importance of learning English to excel in this profession, motivating me to move to the USA for 
language acquisition. 
 
Initially aspiring to be a coder and develop tools to combat cyberattacks, my journey took an 
unexpected turn when the onset of COVID-19 disrupted my coding classes at EMU. This setback led 
me to reassess my career goals, and in discussions with Professor Tout, he introduced me to 
alternative paths, particularly in information assurance and cyber defense, with a focus on 
penetration testing. Despite lacking prior experience in this field, my interest grew as I explored the 
subject through TED Talks and YouTube videos. 
 
A pivotal moment occurred last semester when I took IA 427, where I delved into digital forensics 
using tools like Autopsy. The experience of scrutinizing individuals' machines for information in 
various cases proved to be both enjoyable and fulfilling. Inspired by this, my primary goal now is to 
disseminate information and knowledge about cyberattacks, particularly targeting individuals 
unfamiliar with the intricacies of cybersecurity, such as older demographics. 
 
As I look forward to a career in digital forensics, I have encountered challenges in finding suitable 
job opportunities. Despite my enthusiasm, job searches for positions in this field have been 
discouraging, and I have explored alternatives like pursuing an entry-level position as an SOC 
Analyst I. However, a significant hurdle arises as many positions as possible in the USA require 
citizenship, leaving me uncertain about my prospects post-graduation. This uncertainty raises 
concerns about my future in the field and the potential limitations I may face.
